How to read the results

We list direct provider-to-customer links only, not peers or the full customer cone. A customer is single-homed if the complete relationships snapshot shows one distinct provider ASN, and multi-homed if it shows two or more. Filters never change those counts. Inferred relationships do not prove contracts, physical links, or current connectivity.

Europe follows UN M49 (with Kosovo included for the XK code) and uses the CAIDA organization registration country, not the network’s physical footprint. Unknown countries appear under All regions and are excluded from Europe.

Sources: CAIDA AS Relationships (serial-1) and CAIDA AS Organizations. Prefix-to-AS mappings do not establish provider relationships.
